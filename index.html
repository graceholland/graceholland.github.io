Brand Work

Brand Identity.

Building a brand from zero means making every decision — from the colors that feel right to the words that make people trust you. Here's what went into OKBioStart.

OKBioStart launched in 2023 with no existing brand identity. I developed everything — visual language, voice, messaging, and the guidelines to keep it consistent as the team grew.

The brand needed to feel both scientific and approachable, innovative but not intimidating. The result: bold typography, a confident purple-forward palette, and a voice that's described as "confident, smart, helpful, and quick to innovate."

Logo System
Developed primary and secondary logo variants with four approved background treatments — black, purple, magenta, and white. Established clear-space rules and a favicon mark for digital use.
02
Typography Hierarchy
Selected Arboria (Book Black + Light variants) as the primary typeface — modern and scientific without feeling cold. Established clear usage rules for headlines, body copy, and sub-headings.
03
Brand Voice & Messaging
Defined brand voice as "confident, smart, helpful, inviting, collaborative, clear, quick to innovate." Articulated 10 brand values and 10 emotional feelings the brand should evoke in every stakeholder interaction.
04
Collateral & Templates
Applied the brand across letterhead, event flyers, presentation decks, one-pagers, media advisories, hiring materials, and the 2024 Impact Report — maintaining visual consistency across all 8+ document types.
